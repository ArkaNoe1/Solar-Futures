Connecting with the European cultural landscape: SOLAR FUTURES' first online mentoring session

24.07.2026

Alongside the artistic residencies, SOLAR FUTURES includes an online mentoring programme coordinated by project partner LEV, designed to support the artists in developing their projects and navigating the professional contexts in which their work will be presented.

The first session explored the European cultural landscape, with a focus on cultural networks, institutions and EU cultural policies for the 2023–2026 period.

The discussion addressed different aspects of distributing digital and media art, from identifying relevant festivals and institutions to building professional connections and communicating artistic projects to different audiences. The session also touched on questions of authorship and intellectual property, highlighting some of the considerations artists face when sharing and presenting their work.

Through these exchanges, the mentoring programme aims to equip the artists with tools and perspectives that can support the circulation of their projects and strengthen their professional development beyond the residency period.

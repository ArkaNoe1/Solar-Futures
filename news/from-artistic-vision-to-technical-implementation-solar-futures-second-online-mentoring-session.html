From artistic vision to technical implementation: SOLAR FUTURES' second online mentoring session

27.10.2026

As part of the online mentoring programme coordinated by project partner LEV, the second SOLAR FUTURES session focused on the practical aspects of presenting and distributing artistic projects, with particular attention to technical riders.

The session explored how artists can communicate the technical requirements of their work clearly, covering good practices, common challenges and the different needs that may arise across exhibition contexts. It also addressed how to balance artistic ambitions with the technical and practical constraints of each venue, helping artists prepare their projects for future presentations.

Another key topic was the importance of making artistic knowledge more accessible. The artists were invited to share open resources related to their works — such as research materials, circuit diagrams or code — so that audiences and other practitioners can explore their processes, learn from them and build upon them.

The session also included an exchange on the current development of the three artworks, with individual follow-up discussions planned to address each project's specific needs.

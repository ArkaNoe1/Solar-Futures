SOLAR FUTURES featured in HACNUmedia

12.11.2026

HACNUMedia features SOLAR FUTURES and the work of Romanian artist David Lazar in its article “Solarpunk, futuristic visions that stand against dystopia”, exploring how artistic and technological practices can contribute to imagining more sustainable futures.

The article includes a look at inference_commons and the project's approach to rethinking our relationship with technology, digital infrastructures and ecology.

Introducing the SOLAR FUTURES Ecosystem

09.06.2026

We were thrilled by the response to the SOLAR FUTURES Open Call. We received applications from a wide range of artists exploring themes closely connected to Solarpunk, regenerative futures, sustainability, technology, and new ways of imagining our collective future.

Although only three artists could be selected for the residency programme, we wanted to find a way to give visibility to the wider community of practitioners working in this space.

To acknowledge and celebrate this wider network, we have created a new section on our website: Ecosystem.

Here you'll find a growing list of artists whose work resonates with the themes and values of SOLAR FUTURES. We hope this space helps create new connections, inspires future collaborations, and serves as a resource for anyone interested in creative approaches to ecological and social transformation.

Take a look and discover the many artists helping to imagine more regenerative futures.
